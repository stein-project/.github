# Stein Trademark Policy

The Stein **name and logo** belong to Breno Xavier ([github.com/brenoxavier](https://github.com/brenoxavier)).
This page explains how you can use them. In short: talk about Stein freely, build mods for it freely, but don't
put the Stein name on something that isn't Stein.

The marks include the names "Stein", "Stein Client", "Stein Loader", "Stein Bridge", "Stein GFX", "Stein SDK",
"Stein Bot" and "Stein Labs", and the Stein logo and icons.

No licence of any Stein component (MPL-2.0 or MIT) grants rights to these marks.

## Allowed without asking

- **Talking about Stein**: articles, videos, streams, reviews, tutorials and screenshots.
- **Describing your own work truthfully**: "a mod for Stein Loader", "compatible with Stein", "made with the Stein
  SDK", "based on the Stein HUD mod".
- **Linking to the official downloads** (the Stein Client and the SDK releases).

## Not allowed

- Using the marks in the name of your own product, or a confusingly similar name or logo.
- Using the marks in a way that suggests your project is official, endorsed by Stein or made by its author when it
  isn't.
- Distributing anything under the Stein name or logo that is not an official, unmodified Stein release. The Stein
  Client itself may only be redistributed with written permission (see its licence).

## If you fork a Stein component

The open-source components (Stein Bridge, Stein Loader, the installer and the mods) are under the MPL-2.0, which lets
you change and distribute them. Your fork must:

1. **Use a different name and logo**, everywhere users see it.
2. **Credit the original**, visibly, with a link back: "Based on the Stein HUD mod by Breno Xavier —
   https://github.com/Stein-Project".
3. **Keep the licence and copyright notices** the MPL-2.0 requires.

Internal identifiers such as package names (`dev.xavier.stein.*`), mod ids and config file names may stay as they
are: users don't see them, and renaming them would break compatibility.

## Everything else: ask

For any use not covered here, including shipping Stein inside another client, launcher or modpack, write to
[x4vieer@proton.me](mailto:x4vieer@proton.me). Permission is given in writing, case by case.
